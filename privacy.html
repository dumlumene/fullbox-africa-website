Privacy Policy

The Fullbox Africa website provides information about fulfillment services and accepts merchant enquiries.

Information we receive

When you submit an enquiry, we receive your business and contact details, company website, target markets, projected volumes, service requirements and any additional information you provide. Please avoid sending payment details, identity documents or customer data through the form.

How we use and share it

We use enquiry information to respond, assess your business requirements and follow up on the requested services. Website hosting, spam prevention and lead intake providers may process information to deliver those services. The Marketplace early-access form is hosted by Tally and is subject to its own privacy information.

Cookies and analytics

The corporate website does not set advertising cookies. Cloudflare Turnstile performs a security check when online enquiries are enabled. If enabled, Vercel Web Analytics measures visits and enquiry conversions without sending form contents.

Privacy requests

Use the website contact section to reach us about your information. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information or object to certain processing.
